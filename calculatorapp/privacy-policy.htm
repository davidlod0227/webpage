Privacy Policy

CalculatorApp

Effective date: 20 November 2026

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how CalculatorApp handles user information.

CalculatorApp is a calculator application designed to perform mathematical calculations directly on your device. We respect your privacy. CalculatorApp does not collect, transmit, or share personal information.

2. Information Collection

CalculatorApp does not collect or request personal information, including names, email addresses, telephone numbers, contact lists, or location information.

The application does not require users to register an account or sign in.

3. How the Application Works

Mathematical calculations are processed locally on your device.

CalculatorApp does not send calculation inputs, results, or usage information to external servers.

The application does not use advertising services, analytics services, or third-party crash-reporting services.

4. Information Sharing

CalculatorApp does not sell, share, or disclose users' personal information to third parties.

The application does not transmit personal information to the developer or any external service.

5. Data Storage and Retention

The developer does not receive or maintain a database of users' personal information.

Any information processed by the application remains on the user's device and is not transmitted to the developer.

The developer does not retain personal information collected through the application.

6. Data Security

As CalculatorApp does not transmit personal information to external servers, the developer does not maintain a server-side database of user information.

Users should maintain appropriate security on their own devices.

7. Children's Privacy

CalculatorApp does not knowingly collect personal information from children or other users.

The application does not require users to provide personal information.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated if the application's features or privacy practices change.

Any updated version will be published at the same privacy policy webpage.
